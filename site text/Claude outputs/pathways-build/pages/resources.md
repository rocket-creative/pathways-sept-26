# Resources for Clients

This is the practice's library: the handouts, worksheets, and guides our clinicians hand to clients after sessions, collected in one place so you can download them when you need them. Every item names the clinician who wrote it and the date it was last reviewed. Nothing here replaces care. It supports it.

[RESOURCE LIBRARY]

## How this library works

Items are grouped by topic and filterable by pillar. Each resource has its own page with a short description, the download, and links to the services and concerns it relates to. Your clinician may point you to a specific item after a session. You can also browse on your own.

Suggested starter set based on what the team has discussed: a first session checklist, a grounding skills card, a sleep hygiene handout, a pre op bariatric evaluation prep sheet, a parent guide to PCIT PRIDE skills, a what to expect before your first acupuncture visit sheet, a medication questions worksheet to bring to your prescriber appointment.]

## Topics

- Starting care: what to expect, what to bring, how to prepare
- Anxiety and stress: grounding, breathing, and thought records
- Trauma: safety planning and between session skills
- Parents and families: PCIT skills, teen conversations, school support
- Medication: questions to ask, side effect tracking, refill timing
- Wellness: before and after care for massage, acupuncture, and cupping
- Crisis and support lines: 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988; Veterans press 1), [Veterans Crisis Line](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/), [SAMHSA National Helpline](https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline) 1-800-662-4357
