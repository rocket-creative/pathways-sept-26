# Insurance and Fees

Pathways Within works with most major insurance plans for therapy and medication management, accepts NYSHIP for eligible medical massage, and holds a limited number of sliding scale spots for people paying out of pocket. Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan. You do not have to sort it out before you call. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits after your 360 intake and tells you what to expect before your first appointment.

## How verification works

1. You contact the Welcome Team and tell them your plan, or that you are not insured, or that you are not sure.
2. After your 360 intake, the team checks your benefits for the services in your plan: therapy, medication management, acupuncture, or medical massage.
3. Before your first appointment, they tell you what your plan covers, what your estimated share is, and whether any referral or authorization is needed.
4. If something changes with your coverage later, tell the team and they will recheck.

Insurance is a question we answer, not a gate you have to pass.

## Out of network and self pay

If your plan is out of network for a provider, or you prefer not to use insurance, you pay at the time of service and we provide a superbill you can submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. ## Sliding scale

We hold a limited number of sliding scale spots based on financial need and availability. If you are paying out of pocket and cost is a concern, say so when you call. The goal is to make care possible, and the team will tell you plainly what is available.

## Common questions

### Do I need to know my benefits before my first call?

No. Tell the Welcome Team the name of your plan if you know it. They do the checking after your intake.

### What if my therapist is out of network but I still want to see them?

You can. You pay the session rate and receive a superbill to submit to your plan. Many PPO plans reimburse part of out of network therapy.

### Does insurance cover medication management?

Most plans that cover mental health care cover psychiatric evaluation and medication management visits. Coverage for specific medications depends on your pharmacy benefit.

### Does insurance cover massage or acupuncture?

Massage is covered for eligible NYSHIP members as medical massage. Acupuncture coverage depends on the plan; the Welcome Team can check. Energy work and coaching are self pay.

### Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Yes, for therapy, medication management, and eligible wellness services. Ask the Welcome Team if you are unsure whether a service qualifies.
