# Our Long Island Offices

Pathways Within has five offices across Nassau and Suffolk County, plus telehealth for clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. Therapy is offered at every office. Medication management and wellness services such as massage and acupuncture are offered at select offices, so each location page lists exactly what is available there.

You do not have to pick an office before you reach out. The Welcome Team asks where you live and work during your 360 intake and matches you with a provider at the office that fits. Many clients mix in person visits with video sessions. Read more at [how it works](/how-it-works).

## Our offices

[LOCATION CARDS: rockville-centre, garden-city, massapequa, smithtown, port-jefferson]

- [Rockville Centre](/locations/rockville-centre): our flagship, with therapy, medication management, and wellness under one roof. Moving to the full third floor at 53 N Park Ave, target opening September 1, 2026.
- [Garden City](/locations/garden-city): therapy and medication management at 647 Franklin Ave, with wellness services being added.
- [Massapequa](/locations/massapequa): therapy and medication management at 4160 Merrick Road.
- [Smithtown](/locations/smithtown): our Suffolk County hub at 496 Smithtown Bypass, with therapy, medication management, and wellness.
- [Port Jefferson](/locations/port-jefferson): therapy at 1227 Main Street on the North Shore. [NEEDS: confirm this office is open]

## Accessibility

Two of our offices are wheelchair accessible. Rockville Centre has elevator access to the third floor [NEEDS: confirm], and Smithtown has an accessible entrance [NEEDS: confirm ground floor or elevator]. Garden City and Massapequa are reached by stairs and are not wheelchair accessible. Port Jefferson access is being confirmed. [NEEDS: accessibility for Port Jefferson]

If stairs are a barrier, tell the Welcome Team when you book. They will schedule you at Rockville Centre or Smithtown, or match you with a provider who offers telehealth.

## Telehealth

Most of our therapists and our psychiatric nurse practitioner see clients by video. Telehealth is available to clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. Wellness services such as massage, acupuncture, and cryotherapy are in person only. Learn more on our [telehealth](/telehealth) page.
