# IFS Therapy on Long Island

Internal Family Systems (IFS) therapy is an evidence based approach that helps adults and teens explore and heal the many "parts" within them: the protectors, the exiled wounds, and the Self that holds wisdom and compassion. Therapists trained in IFS at Pathways Within offer it in person on Long Island and by telehealth.

## What IFS is

IFS is a therapeutic model based on the idea that our minds are made up of different parts, each with its own feelings, beliefs, and roles. Some parts work to protect us. Others carry pain. Some simply get stuck in old patterns. IFS helps you understand these parts and build a compassionate relationship with them, guided by your inner Self, a calm, wise, and centered presence within you.

At Pathways Within, IFS is a gentle way to deepen your understanding of your inner life. It does not treat any part of you as a problem to remove. Rather than trying to "fix" you, we support you in developing a more compassionate relationship with all of your parts, even the ones that feel painful, confusing, or hard to face.

Many therapies focus on changing thoughts or behaviors. IFS focuses on internal relationships: how your different parts interact, and how your Self can step in as a compassionate leader. The goal is not to get rid of parts but to understand and heal them so they can take on healthier roles.

## Who it helps

IFS therapy can help if you:

- Struggle with overwhelming emotions or internal conflict
- Feel stuck in patterns of [self criticism](/concerns/self-esteem), avoidance, or shame
- Want to explore the roots of [trauma](/concerns/ptsd), [anxiety](/concerns/anxiety), or [depression](/concerns/depression)
- Have difficulty making decisions or trusting your intuition
- Are curious about deep personal growth

Many of our clients find IFS especially effective for trauma work. Unlike traditional talk therapy, IFS lets you engage with traumatic memories from a place of emotional safety. You will not be asked to relive painful moments. Instead, you learn to connect with the parts of you that carry pain or fear, understand their roles, and help them heal.

This approach is particularly supportive for survivors of childhood abuse, complex trauma, or situations where traditional therapy has felt too overwhelming or ineffective. If you are seeking a gentle way to work through trauma, IFS offers a path forward.

## What to expect in your first session at Pathways Within

Your therapist helps you slow down and gently explore your inner world. In the first session, that starts with what brought you in and what you hope will change. There is no pressure to relive trauma or rush the process. IFS is paced to match your comfort and readiness.

In later sessions, you might be invited to focus on a specific feeling, behavior, or inner voice, and to get to know the part of you behind it. Your therapist guides you in noticing what is happening inside and learning how to relate to your inner experience with care and curiosity.

Over time, this process brings greater clarity, calm, and connection, within yourself and in your relationships with others. Sessions run about 50 minutes. You set the depth and the pace.

## Common questions

### What is Internal Family Systems (IFS) therapy?

IFS is a therapeutic model based on the idea that our minds are made up of different parts, each with its own feelings, beliefs, and roles. Some parts protect us, others carry pain, and some get stuck in old patterns. IFS helps you understand these parts and build a compassionate relationship with them, guided by your inner Self.

### What does an IFS session look like?

Your therapist helps you slow down and gently explore your inner world. You might be invited to focus on a specific feeling, behavior, or inner voice, and to get to know the part of you behind it. There is no pressure to relive trauma or rush the process. IFS is paced to match your comfort and readiness.

### Is IFS only for people with trauma?

Not at all. While IFS is highly effective for trauma work, it is also helpful for anyone who feels stuck in repetitive emotional patterns, internal conflict, or self criticism. Many people use IFS to deepen self awareness, reduce anxiety, or connect more fully with their intuition and values.

### Will I have to "perform" or go deep right away?

No. IFS is based on the principle that your system knows what it needs and when it is ready. You will never be pushed to go deeper than you feel comfortable. Your therapist meets you where you are and creates a safe space to explore at your own pace.

### How is IFS different from other types of therapy?

Many therapies focus on changing thoughts or behaviors. IFS focuses on internal relationships, how your different parts interact, and how your Self can step in as a compassionate leader. The goal is not to get rid of parts but to understand and heal them so they can take on healthier roles.
