# Therapists Who Accept Oscar on Long Island

Oscar plans are on the Pathways Within intake form, and our clinicians see Oscar clients for therapy and medication management. If you are new to your plan and not sure how therapy fits into it, this page is for you. Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits after your 360 intake and tells you what applies before your first appointment.

[CTA] Start your 360 intake -> /contact

## What Oscar may cover here

Your Oscar plan may cover [individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy), [medication management](/medication-management) with our psychiatric nurse practitioner, and, depending on the plan, [couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy). The Welcome Team verifies each service on its own, because therapy and medication visits involve different providers and are sometimes covered differently.

Which of our clinicians are in network with Oscar is a separate question from whether the practice works with Oscar. Not every therapist participates with every plan. The Welcome Team matches you with a provider who does, and then confirms the plan's terms. We do not publish copays, deductibles, or session limits on this page because they are specific to your plan and change over time. The practice wide overview is at [insurance and fees](/insurance-and-fees).

## How verification works

Care comes first and the benefits check follows. Nobody is turned away at the first call for not knowing their coverage.

1. Contact the Welcome Team by form, call, or text.
2. A Welcome Team member schedules your 360 intake, a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you want to change.
3. The team builds a care plan and matches you with available providers who work with Oscar.
4. The Welcome Team verifies your Oscar benefits and explains what to expect before your first appointment.

Your Welcome Team contact stays with you after that. See [How it works](/how-it-works) for the full picture.

## Out of network and self pay options

If your Oscar plan is out of network for the clinician you want, or does not cover a service, the Welcome Team says so before anything is scheduled. Self pay is available for every service at the practice. We accept cash, major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. A limited number of sliding scale spots are available based on need and availability.

If you want to file for out of network reimbursement yourself, ask the Welcome Team about a superbill.

## Locations and telehealth

Oscar clients are seen in person at Rockville Centre, Garden City, Massapequa, Smithtown, and Port Jefferson. The [locations](/locations) page has addresses and accessibility details for each office.

Telehealth is available to clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. Many Oscar clients prefer video sessions, and the Welcome Team confirms how your plan treats them during verification.

## Common questions

### Does Pathways Within take Oscar for telehealth?

Telehealth is offered, and whether your Oscar plan covers it the same way as an office visit depends on the plan. The Welcome Team checks that specifically.

### Do I need a referral or prior authorization?

Some plans require one for outpatient mental health and some do not. The Welcome Team confirms which applies before your first appointment and handles any request with the provider.

### Can I switch from self pay to Oscar later, or the other way?

Yes. If your coverage changes, tell the Welcome Team and they will verify the new plan. Your care does not have to stop while that happens.
