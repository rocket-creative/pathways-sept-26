# Therapists Who Accept Aetna on Long Island

Pathways Within works with Aetna for therapy and medication management at our Long Island offices and by telehealth. Aetna is one of the plans listed on our intake form, and many of our clients use it. Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan, so the Welcome Team verifies your benefits after your 360 intake rather than asking you to figure it out alone.

[CTA] Start your 360 intake -> /contact

## What Aetna may cover here

Most Aetna plans include some level of outpatient mental health benefit. At Pathways Within, that benefit may apply to [individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy) with a licensed clinician, [medication management](/medication-management) with our psychiatric nurse practitioner, and [couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy), depending on how your plan defines each service.

Two things shape what you actually pay. The first is whether the specific clinician you see is in network with your Aetna plan. Not every therapist at the practice participates with every plan. The second is the details of your own plan, including any cost sharing that applies to outpatient visits. The Welcome Team checks both before your first appointment so nothing surprises you.

We do not quote copays, deductibles, or session limits on this page. Those numbers belong to your plan, and they change. Read our full [insurance and fees](/insurance-and-fees) page for how billing works across the practice.

## How verification works

You do not need to know your benefits to reach out. Here is the order of events.

1. You contact the Welcome Team by form, call, or text.
2. A Welcome Team member schedules your 360 intake, a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you want to change.
3. The team builds a care plan and matches you with available providers who work with Aetna.
4. The Welcome Team verifies your Aetna benefits and tells you what to expect before your first appointment.

There is no insurance gate at the front door. We match you to care first, then confirm how it is paid for. The whole process is described at [How it works](/how-it-works).

## Out of network and self pay options

If your Aetna plan turns out to be out of network for the clinician you want, or for a service like couples therapy, you still have options. Self pay is available for every service at the practice. We accept cash, major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. A limited number of sliding scale spots are available based on need and availability.

The Welcome Team can also tell you whether a superbill for out of network reimbursement makes sense for your plan. Ask during your intake and they will walk you through it.

## Locations and telehealth

You can use your Aetna coverage in person at any of our Long Island offices: Rockville Centre, Garden City, Massapequa, Smithtown, and Port Jefferson. See all [locations](/locations) for addresses and accessibility.

Telehealth is available to clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. Whether a telehealth session is covered the same way as an office visit depends on your plan, and the Welcome Team checks that too.

## Common questions

### Does every therapist at Pathways Within take Aetna?

No. Participation varies by clinician. When you tell the Welcome Team you have Aetna, they match you with providers who work with your plan and confirm the match before scheduling.

### Is medication management covered under Aetna?

Many Aetna plans cover psychiatric medication visits, but the benefit depends on the plan and the prescriber's network status. The Welcome Team verifies this separately from therapy benefits.

### What if I have an Aetna plan through my employer?

Employer plans differ from individual plans, sometimes a lot. Bring your card to your intake or share a photo with the Welcome Team. They verify the exact plan you carry rather than assuming.
