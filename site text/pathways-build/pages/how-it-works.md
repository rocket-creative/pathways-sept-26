# How Care Works at Pathways Within

You do not have to pick a service, find a therapist, or check your insurance before you call. You tell the Welcome Team what is going on. They take it from there. This page explains every step so nothing about starting feels like a mystery.

[CTA] Start your 360 intake -> /contact

## Step 1: You reach out

Call or text (631) 371-3825, or fill out the [contact form](/contact). The form asks who you are, how to reach you, which office or telehealth you prefer, what you are interested in, and what you want support with. It also asks about insurance, so the team can check benefits early. Insurance is not a gate. "Not insured" and "not sure" are both fine answers.

## Step 2: The Welcome Team calls you back

A member of the Welcome Team reaches out, usually within one business day [NEEDS: confirm response time]. They are the people who guide everyone through the process, communicate with clinicians, and make sure you get what you came for. They will answer questions and schedule your 360 intake.

## Step 3: Your 360 intake

The 360 intake is a conversation with a clinician, not a form. It covers what you are experiencing across your mind, your body, your relationships, and your daily life. It looks at what has helped before and what has not. It is called 360 because it goes all the way around you, instead of stopping at the one concern that made you call.

## Step 4: Your care plan

From the intake, the team builds a plan. For many people that is one therapist. For others it is a therapist plus [medication management](/medication-management), or therapy plus [massage](/wellness/massage) or [acupuncture](/wellness/acupuncture) for the physical side of stress, or [coaching](/coaching) once the deeper work is done. Nothing is required, and you can say no to any part. Your providers coordinate with your permission.

## Step 5: Your first appointment

The Welcome Team matches you with an available provider at the office you chose or by telehealth, and books your first appointment. They stay your point of contact. If a match is not right, tell them and they will adjust.

## What it costs

Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits after the intake and tells you what to expect before your first appointment. We accept cash, major credit cards, and HSA and FSA funds, and hold a limited number of sliding scale spots based on need. Full details at [insurance and fees](/insurance-and-fees).

## Common questions

### Do I need a referral?

No. You can contact us directly. If your plan requires a referral or authorization (some Medicare and VA Community Care arrangements do), the Welcome Team will tell you what to get and from whom.

### What if I only want massage or acupuncture?

That is fine. Wellness services are open to everyone, whether or not you see a therapist here. Tell the Welcome Team what you want and they will book it.

### Can I choose my therapist?

Yes. If you have a name in mind, put it on the form. If not, the team matches you based on your intake, your schedule, and the office you chose.

### What if I have a question about billing or an appointment?
