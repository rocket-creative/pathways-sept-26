# What We Treat on Long Island

Most people do not search for a therapy modality. They search for what hurts. This page lists the concerns we treat most often, what we do for each, and who you might work with. Every page below is written and reviewed by a licensed clinician and links to the services, providers, and offices that apply.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Veterans: press 1) or call 911.

## Mood and anxiety

- [Anxiety](/concerns/anxiety): worry, panic, and the physical tension that comes with them.
- [Depression](/concerns/depression): low mood, loss of interest, and the days that feel heavy.
- [Bipolar disorder](/concerns/bipolar-disorder): mood cycles managed with medication and therapy together.
- [OCD](/concerns/ocd): intrusive thoughts and the rituals that follow them.
- [Stress and burnout](/concerns/stress-and-burnout): when work or caregiving has taken more than it gives back.

## Trauma and loss

- [Trauma and PTSD](/concerns/ptsd): single incident and complex trauma, with EMDR, IFS, and somatic options.
- [Grief and loss](/concerns/grief-and-loss): after a death, a diagnosis, a divorce, or any ending.

## Attention and development

- [ADHD](/concerns/adhd): for children, teens, and adults, including medication management from age 10.

## Relationships and life

- [Relationship issues](/concerns/relationship-issues): couples, families, and the patterns that repeat.
- [Life transitions](/concerns/life-transitions): divorce, career change, college, retirement, new parenthood.
- [Self esteem](/concerns/self-esteem): the inner critic and where it came from.
- [LGBTQIA+ affirming therapy](/concerns/lgbtqia-affirming-therapy): care where your identity is not the problem.
- [Postpartum and perinatal](/concerns/postpartum-and-perinatal): pregnancy, birth, and the first year.

## Body and health

- [Chronic pain and illness](/concerns/chronic-pain-and-illness): therapy, acupuncture, and massage for the whole picture.
- [Substance use](/concerns/substance-use): outpatient support alongside recovery.

## Not on this list?

That happens often. Our clinicians also list specialties such as anger, school refusal, autism, neurodivergence, men's issues, domestic violence recovery, sexual assault recovery, borderline personality disorder, and midlife changes. Search [providers](/providers) by specialty, or just tell the Welcome Team what is going on. The 360 intake is built for concerns that do not fit a label. [How it works](/how-it-works).
