# Therapists Who Accept Northwell Direct on Long Island

If Northwell Direct is the name on your card, Pathways Within can see you. It is one of the plans listed on our intake form, and our licensed therapists and psychiatric nurse practitioner work with Northwell Direct clients in person across Nassau and Suffolk County and by telehealth. Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits after your 360 intake and tells you what applies before your first session.

[CTA] Start your 360 intake -> /contact

## What Northwell Direct may cover here

Your Northwell Direct plan may cover [individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy), [medication management](/medication-management), and, depending on the plan, [couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy). The Welcome Team verifies each service separately. Therapy and medication visits involve different providers and can sit under different parts of the same benefit.

Which of our clinicians participate with Northwell Direct is a per provider question. The Welcome Team matches you with one who does, then confirms the terms of your plan. We do not print copays, deductibles, or visit limits on this page because they belong to your plan and are subject to change. The practice wide overview is on our [insurance and fees](/insurance-and-fees) page.

## How verification works

The first conversation is about what you need, not about your card. Verification follows.

1. You contact the Welcome Team by form, call, or text.
2. A Welcome Team member schedules your 360 intake, a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you want to change.
3. The team builds a care plan across therapy, medication management, and wellness as needed, and matches you with providers who work with Northwell Direct.
4. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits and confirms what applies before your first appointment.

Your Welcome Team contact stays with you from there. Read the full process at [How it works](/how-it-works).

## Out of network and self pay options

If your Northwell Direct plan is out of network for the clinician you want, or does not cover a service, the Welcome Team tells you before you are scheduled. Every service at Pathways Within is available on a self pay basis. We accept cash, major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. A limited number of sliding scale spots are available based on need and availability.

Ask the Welcome Team about a superbill if you want to submit for out of network reimbursement yourself.

## Locations and telehealth

Northwell Direct clients are seen at Rockville Centre, Garden City, Massapequa, Smithtown, and Port Jefferson. Addresses and accessibility notes are on the [locations](/locations) page.

Telehealth is available to clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. The Welcome Team confirms how your plan treats video sessions during verification.

## Common questions

### Is Northwell Direct the same as other Northwell plans?

Not necessarily. Plan names that share a word can have different networks and rules. Send the Welcome Team a photo of your card and they will verify the exact plan you carry.

### Does Northwell Direct cover medication management here?

It may. Psychiatric medication visits are verified separately from therapy, and the answer depends on your plan and the prescriber's network status.

### What if I need prior authorization?

If your plan requires it, the Welcome Team coordinates the request with your provider and tells you where things stand. You will know before your appointment, not after.
