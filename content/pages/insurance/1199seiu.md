# Therapists Who Accept 1199SEIU on Long Island

Many people who work in healthcare on Long Island carry 1199SEIU benefits, and many of them work shifts that make therapy hard to fit in. Pathways Within lists 1199SEIU on its intake form and sees members for therapy and medication management in person and by telehealth, with some evening availability. Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits after your 360 intake.

[CTA] Start your 360 intake -> /contact

## What 1199SEIU may cover here

Your 1199SEIU benefits may apply to [individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy) with a licensed clinician, [medication management](/medication-management) with our psychiatric nurse practitioner, and possibly [couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy). Each is verified on its own because each involves a different provider and may fall under a different part of your benefit.

Whether a particular clinician is in network with 1199SEIU is a per provider question, and the Welcome Team matches you with someone who participates before confirming the details. We keep copays, deductibles, and visit limits off this page. Those belong to your benefit plan and are best confirmed directly. For how billing works across the practice, see [insurance and fees](/insurance-and-fees).

## How verification works

You do not need to bring a benefits summary to your first call. The Welcome Team asks about you first.

1. You contact the Welcome Team by form, call, or text.
2. A Welcome Team member schedules your 360 intake, a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you want to change.
3. The team builds a care plan across therapy, medication management, and wellness as needed, and matches you with providers who work with 1199SEIU.
4. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits and tells you what to expect before your first appointment.

That Welcome Team member stays your point of contact. Read more at [How it works](/how-it-works).

## Out of network and self pay options

If the clinician you want is not in network with 1199SEIU, or a service is not covered, the Welcome Team tells you before you are scheduled. Every service at Pathways Within is available on a self pay basis. We accept cash, major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. A limited number of sliding scale spots are available based on need and availability.

Ask the Welcome Team about a superbill if you plan to seek out of network reimbursement on your own.

## Locations and telehealth

1199SEIU members are seen at Rockville Centre, Garden City, Massapequa, Smithtown, and Port Jefferson. Details for each office, including accessibility, are on the [locations](/locations) page.

Telehealth is available to clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. For members working rotating or overnight shifts, video sessions are often the easiest way to keep therapy steady. The Welcome Team confirms how your plan treats telehealth during verification.

## Common questions

### The intake form says 1199. Is that the same thing?

Yes. The form uses the short name. Select 1199 and the Welcome Team will verify your 1199SEIU benefits.

### Can my family members use my 1199SEIU benefits here?

Whether dependents are covered, and for which services, depends on your specific benefit plan. Tell the Welcome Team who will be seen and they will verify each person.

### Do you have appointments outside regular work hours?

Clinician appointment times vary and may fall outside front desk hours, including evenings. Tell the Welcome Team your shift pattern and they will look for a provider whose schedule fits.

Tell the Welcome Team you have 1199SEIU and what is going on. They will schedule your 360 intake, match you with a provider, and check your benefits.
