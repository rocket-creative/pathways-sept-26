# Therapists Who Accept Medicare on Long Island

Finding a therapist who takes Medicare on Long Island can take longer than it should. Pathways Within lists Medicare on its intake form and sees Medicare clients for therapy and medication management. Not every clinician at the practice is enrolled with Medicare, so the Welcome Team matches you with one who is. Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits after your 360 intake.

## What Medicare may cover here

Medicare may cover [individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy) with a Medicare enrolled clinician and [medication management](/medication-management) with a Medicare enrolled prescriber. [Couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy) is treated differently and may not be covered, so ask the Welcome Team about it directly if that is what you are looking for.

The single most important factor is the enrollment status of the specific provider you see. A clinician who is not enrolled with Medicare cannot bill Medicare for your visit, no matter what your card says. That is why the Welcome Team confirms the match before you are scheduled. We do not state deductibles, cost sharing, or visit limits on this page. Those are set by Medicare and by any supplemental or Advantage plan you carry. See [insurance and fees](/insurance-and-fees) for the practice wide picture.

## How verification works

You do not have to sort out Medicare rules before you call. The 360 intake comes first, and the Welcome Team handles the rest.

1. You contact the Welcome Team by form, call, or text.
2. A Welcome Team member schedules your 360 intake, a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you want to change.
3. The team builds a care plan and matches you with available providers who are enrolled with Medicare.
4. The Welcome Team verifies your Medicare benefits, including any supplemental or Advantage plan, before your first appointment.

The Welcome Team stays your point of contact after that. Read the full process at [How it works](/how-it-works).

## Out of network and self pay options

If the clinician you want is not enrolled with Medicare, or a service is not covered, the Welcome Team tells you before you are scheduled. Self pay is available for every service at Pathways Within. We accept cash, major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. 

Medicare has its own rules about billing clients directly, and the Welcome Team explains what those rules mean for you before any self pay arrangement begins.

## Locations and telehealth

Medicare clients are seen in person at Rockville Centre, Garden City, Massapequa, Smithtown, and Port Jefferson. Accessibility differs by office, and the [locations](/locations) page says which offices have stairs and which do not.

Telehealth is available to clients in New York when a video visit is available. How Medicare treats video visits for mental health is something the Welcome Team confirms during verification rather than something we state here.

## Common questions

### I have a Medicare Advantage plan. Does this page apply?

Partly. Advantage plans have their own networks and their own rules. Tell the Welcome Team the name of the plan on your card and they will verify that plan specifically.

### Do I need a referral from my doctor?

Some plans require one for outpatient mental health and some do not. The Welcome Team checks your plan and tells you before your first appointment.

### Which of your therapists take Medicare?

The list depends on each clinician's enrollment status, which changes as staff join. Tell the Welcome Team you have Medicare and they will match you with an enrolled provider.
