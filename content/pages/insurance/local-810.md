# Therapists Who Accept Local 810 on Long Island

Local 810 members and their families are welcome at Pathways Within. Local 810 is one of the plans on our intake form, and our licensed therapists and psychiatric nurse practitioner see members for therapy and medication management at our Long Island offices and by telehealth. Coverage varies by provider, service, and plan. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits after your 360 intake, so you know what applies before your first appointment.

[CTA] Start your 360 intake -> /contact

## What Local 810 may cover here

Your Local 810 benefits may apply to [individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy), [medication management](/medication-management), and possibly [couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy). Each is verified on its own. Therapy and medication visits involve different providers and may be covered under different terms, and couples therapy is often handled differently from individual sessions.

In network status is a per clinician question. Some of our providers participate with Local 810 and some do not, so the Welcome Team matches you with one who does, then confirms your plan's terms. We do not list copays, deductibles, or visit limits on this page. Those belong to your benefit plan. The practice wide overview is on our [insurance and fees](/insurance-and-fees) page.

## How verification works

You do not need your benefits booklet in hand to make the first call. The Welcome Team asks about you first and handles the paperwork after.

1. You contact the Welcome Team by form, call, or text.
2. A Welcome Team member schedules your 360 intake, a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you want to change.
3. The team builds a care plan across therapy, medication management, and wellness as needed, and matches you with providers who work with Local 810.
4. The Welcome Team verifies your benefits and tells you what to expect before your first appointment.

Your Welcome Team contact stays with you after that. Read the full process at [How it works](/how-it-works).

## Out of network and self pay options

If the clinician you want is not in network with Local 810, or a service is not covered, the Welcome Team tells you before you are scheduled. Every service at Pathways Within is available on a self pay basis. We accept cash, major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. 

If you would like to seek out of network reimbursement on your own, ask the Welcome Team about a superbill.

## Locations and telehealth

Local 810 members are seen at Rockville Centre, Garden City, Massapequa, Smithtown, and Port Jefferson. Addresses and accessibility notes are on the [locations](/locations) page.

Telehealth is available to clients in New York when a video visit is available. For members with long or irregular work hours, video sessions can make it easier to keep appointments. The Welcome Team confirms how your plan treats telehealth.

## Common questions

### Can my spouse or children use my Local 810 benefits here?

Whether dependents are covered, and for which services, depends on your benefit plan. Tell the Welcome Team who will be seen and they will verify each person separately.

### Do you have appointments after work?

Appointment times vary by clinician. Tell the Welcome Team what times work for you and they will look for a provider whose schedule fits.

### What should I send the Welcome Team?

A photo of the front and back of your insurance card is enough to start. If you have a benefits summary, send that too, but it is not required.

## Take the next step

Tell the Welcome Team you have Local 810 and what you want to change. They will schedule your 360 intake, match you with a provider, and verify your benefits.
