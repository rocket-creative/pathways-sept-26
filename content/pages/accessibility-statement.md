# Accessibility Statement

Pathways Within - Wisdom and Wellness Collaborative wants every person to be able to use this website and to reach our care. This page says what we have done, where we fall short, and how to tell us about a barrier.

## The website

This site is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. In practice that means: text meets contrast minimums; every image has a text description; every page can be used with a keyboard alone; headings are in order; animations stop when your device asks for reduced motion; forms have labels; and text scales with your browser settings. We test with screen readers and keyboard navigation before each release. [NEEDS: date of last audit and tool used]

Our contact forms are provided by a third party platform inside an iframe. If a form does not work with your assistive technology, call or text (631) 371-3825 and the Welcome Team will take your information by phone.
