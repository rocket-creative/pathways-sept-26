# Family Therapy on Long Island

Family therapy at Pathways Within is counseling for biological, blended, and chosen families who want to heal the relationships that shape them most. Licensed family therapists see families in person across Nassau and Suffolk County and by telehealth. Healing happens in connection, and every voice in your family gets heard.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Veterans: press 1) or call 911.

## What family therapy is

Family therapy supports healing within the relationships that shape us most, whether those are with biological family, chosen family, or the unique constellation of people who make up your support system.

We understand that "family" does not look the same for everyone. That is why our approach is flexible, inclusive, and grounded in deep respect for your lived experience. Whether you are facing generational conflict, setting new boundaries, or working through a crisis, our goal is the same. We help your family system move toward healthier patterns and deeper connection.

Your therapist works with your family to create a space where every voice can be heard and respected. Together you explore the patterns that keep you stuck and build tools to help your family relate in more open, authentic, and compassionate ways.

## Who it helps

Family therapy can help with:

- Communication breakdowns and chronic misunderstandings
- Major [life transitions](/concerns/life-transitions) or losses
- Blended family or coparenting challenges
- Cultural tension within the family
- Building stronger bonds
- Supporting a struggling member
- Intergenerational trauma
- [Relationship issues](/concerns/relationship-issues) between parents, partners, siblings, or generations

### Support for multigenerational and chosen families

We work with all types of families, including those with multiple generations living together and families formed through community, friendship, or shared identity. These relationships can bring deep meaning, and sometimes unique challenges.

Our therapists are attuned to the dynamics that emerge across age groups, cultural perspectives, and identity based differences. We help families work through these complexities with compassion, humility, and a commitment to helping each member feel seen, heard, and valued.

When a child or teen is at the center of the concern, your therapist may pair family sessions with [child therapy](/therapy/child-therapy) or [teen therapy](/therapy/teen-therapy). When the strain sits between two partners, [couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy) may be the better starting point.

## What to expect in your first session at Pathways Within

Your first session is a chance for your therapist to meet the family and hear how each person sees what is happening. Everyone gets a turn. Your therapist listens for patterns, strengths, and where communication tends to break down.

From there, you set goals together. Sessions may involve everyone, smaller groupings, or individual meetings as needed. Your therapist explains the plan and adjusts it as your family changes. Sessions run about 50 minutes.

## How care works here

Every family starts with a 360 intake with our Welcome Team. That conversation covers what is happening in the family system and what each member is carrying on their own. The team then matches you with an available family therapist and builds a whole person care plan.

When it helps, that plan can include individual sessions for one member, [medication management](/medication-management) with our psychiatric nurse practitioner, or wellness services for the stress that lives in the body. Nothing is required, and everything is coordinated with your permission. Read the full process at [How it works](/how-it-works), or see all of our [therapy services](/therapy).

## Where it is offered

Family therapy is available in person at all of our Long Island offices and by telehealth to clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. Telehealth can bring together family members who live in different places.

## Common questions

### Who should attend family therapy?

Anyone who is part of your family system. This might include parents, children, partners, siblings, grandparents, or chosen family. We welcome all configurations and family structures. Therapy may involve everyone together, smaller groupings, or individual meetings as needed.

### What if not everyone in the family wants to come?

That is okay. It is common for some members to feel unsure about therapy. You can begin with those who are open to participating. Often, as changes start happening, others become more willing to join. Your therapist helps you handle those dynamics with care.

### What kinds of issues does family therapy address?

Family therapy can help with a wide range of concerns. These include communication challenges, parenting stress, loss, transitions such as divorce or blending families, mental health concerns affecting one or more members, and cultural or intergenerational conflict. We also work with families who want to strengthen their bond before a problem arises.

### Do you work with chosen or nontraditional families?

Absolutely. We honor all family identities, whether related by blood, marriage, adoption, or shared life. Chosen families, polyamorous constellations, LGBTQIA+ families, and other nontraditional structures are always welcome here.

### What is the role of the therapist in family therapy?

Your therapist is a neutral guide who helps your family understand patterns, identify strengths, and improve communication. We do not take sides or assign blame. Instead, we create space for each person to be heard, and for the family to practice new ways of connecting and growing together.
