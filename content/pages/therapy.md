Begin your journey to wellness, where our expert team of therapist’s wisdom guides the way

Have you ever experienced an event that completely changed your view of the world?

Together we can explore your experiences in order to help you feel safe and secure again, and we can develop new styles of communication that can help you create the relationships you have always wanted. Whatever you may be dealing with, we can help.

This means we offer traditional therapy services like individual and couples therapy, along with whole body services, such as massage and acupuncture.

## Therapy for who you are

- [Individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy): Individual therapy is your classic one-on-one counseling. The goal is to find greater self-awareness and give you the tools to live a more meaningful life.
- [Couples therapy](/therapy/couples-therapy): Relationships can be difficult to maintain, especially as the years go by and each person changes in unforeseen ways. A break in communication can often lead to other problems that plague modern marriages, and therapy can help.
- [Child therapy](/therapy/child-therapy): There’s no singular way forward when someone small is experiencing something really big. Play therapy is most often used with children from ages 3 to around 12.
- [Teen therapy](/therapy/teen-therapy): Teens reign supreme in the realm of “I don’t want to talk about it” and with good reason. A comfortable and safe therapeutic relationship can help make sense of the kaleidoscope of experiences that can make teenhood feel tumultuous.
- [Family therapy](/therapy/family-therapy): Family therapy supports healing within the relationships that shape us most, whether those are with biological family, chosen family, or the people who make up your support system.
- [Group therapy](/therapy/group-therapy): While group therapy might sound scary or overwhelming at first, the reality is that we yearn for meaningful connections. Sitting in a room with strangers is the ideal way to share your concerns without feeling judged.
- [Veterans and first responders](/therapy/veterans-first-responders): The very experiences that set you apart as an honorable part of our society can make it feel difficult to participate in society as a whole person when you feel like you’ve been taken apart.
- [Weight loss surgery support](/therapy/bariatric-surgery-support): Weight loss surgery comes with a lot of caveats, and an often overlooked part of the procedure is its psychological effects. Support is here before the decision and after the procedure.

## Therapy for what happened

- [Trauma therapy](/therapy/trauma-therapy): Trauma has lasting physical and emotional effects that can leave you tired, jumpy, anxious, feeling ashamed, exposed, insecure, and wondering who you can trust.
- [EMDR therapy](/therapy/emdr): EMDR can help you heal without spending a lot of time rehashing the things that hurt you. Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing is often used for PTSD and other trigger-based trauma.
- [IFS therapy](/therapy/ifs): Internal Family Systems (IFS) therapy helps you explore and heal the many parts within you: the protectors, exiled wounds, and the Self that holds wisdom and compassion.
- [Somatic therapy](/therapy/somatic-therapy): Our bodies have eternal memories. Whether we remember our experiences or not, they live in our nervous system, and somatic therapy works with what the body holds.
- [Grief therapy](/therapy/grief-therapy): We are not always prepared to deal with losing a loved one, or with the intensity of grief and its ability to take over our lives. Grief therapy gives you time and space to talk through the loss and build coping skills.
- [Hypnotherapy](/therapy/hypnotherapy): Hypnotherapy is the intentional induction of a gentle trance-like state to allow your unconscious mind some time in the driver’s seat.
- [Parent-Child Interaction Therapy](/therapy/pcit): For children and their caregivers who have experienced trauma or are having challenges with behaviors or habits in the home. PCIT helps parents learn skills that give children a more stable foundation.

[Medication management](/medication-management) and [performance and wellness coaching](/coaching) sit alongside therapy when a care plan needs them. For massage, acupuncture, and energy work, see [wellness](/wellness).

## How therapy starts here

You contact the Welcome Team. They schedule a 360 intake, a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you want to change. From that, the team matches you with a therapist whose training fits, at an office or by telehealth in New York. If medication or wellness care would help, it is added to the same plan.
