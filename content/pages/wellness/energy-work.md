# Energy Work on Long Island

Energy work is a gentle, hands on practice that clients use for deep relaxation, emotional processing, and a sense of balance. At Pathways Within, Tia Baumohl, Certified Coach and Energy Medicine Practitioner, offers three modalities: Reiki, Integrated Therapeutic Alignment (ITA), and Integrative Energy Therapy (IET). Sessions are open to anyone, with or without therapy.

Energy work is not a substitute for medical or mental health treatment.

## What energy work is

Energy work covers a family of practices in which a trained practitioner uses light touch, or hands held just above the body, while you rest fully clothed on a treatment table. The practitioner works through a sequence of positions from head to feet. The room is quiet, and there is nothing you need to do except settle in.

Each modality has its own lineage and structure. What they share is a calm, unhurried setting and an invitation to notice what you feel in your body. Tia may combine approaches within one session based on what you are working on and what you prefer. Clients describe the experience as restful, grounding, and sometimes emotional in a way that feels like release rather than distress.

## The three modalities we offer

### Reiki

Reiki is a technique developed in Japan that uses light touch or hovering hands over areas of the body. It is not religious in nature, so it suits people of any faith and people who are not religious. Clients often report warmth, tingling, or a deep sense of calm, and some fall asleep on the table. Read more about [Reiki at Pathways Within](/wellness/energy-work/reiki).

### Integrated Therapeutic Alignment (ITA)

Integrated Therapeutic Alignment is a gentle, energy based practice that works with key connection points on the body. The aim is to help the body, mind, emotions, and energy system settle back into balance. During an ITA session, Tia moves through those points with light touch while you rest.

Clients use ITA when they feel stuck in a stress pattern, a limiting belief, or an emotion that keeps coming back. Many report feeling calmer, clearer, and more at ease afterward. ### Integrative Energy Therapy (IET)

IET is a hands on practice in which the practitioner works with specific points on the body linked to emotional themes. Clients use it for relaxation, emotional processing, and support during life transitions. Read more about [IET at Pathways Within](/wellness/energy-work/iet).

## Who it helps

People come to energy work for many reasons. Common ones include:

- Ongoing stress and the feeling of never fully powering down ([stress and burnout](/concerns/stress-and-burnout))
- A busy or anxious mind that makes rest hard ([anxiety](/concerns/anxiety))
- Big changes such as a move, a loss, a new baby, or a career shift ([life transitions](/concerns/life-transitions))
- Wanting a quiet, body based complement to talk therapy or [coaching](/coaching)
- Curiosity about a spiritual or reflective practice that is not tied to any religion

Energy work is not a treatment for any medical or mental health condition. If you are in therapy, taking medication, or under a doctor's care, continue that care. Energy work sits alongside it.

## What to expect in your first session at Pathways Within

Your first session begins with a short conversation. Tia asks what brought you in, what you hope to feel different, and whether there are areas of the body you prefer not to be touched. You can choose light touch or hands held above the body at any point.

You then lie down fully clothed on a treatment table, usually on your back with a blanket. Tia works through a series of hand positions from head to feet. Sessions typically run about an hour. [NEEDS: confirm session length and pricing for each energy work modality]

People often report feeling heat or tingling where Tia is working, seeing colors, or having memories surface. Some drift into a dreamlike state or fall asleep. Afterward, Tia checks in with you, and you can share as much or as little as you like. Drink water and give yourself a little quiet time before returning to your day.

## Common questions

### Do I have to believe in energy work for it to help?

No. You only need to be willing to lie down and rest. Many clients come in curious or skeptical and simply notice that they feel calmer afterward. Tia does not ask you to adopt any belief system, and the practices are not religious.

### Which modality should I choose?

You do not need to decide before you book. Tell the Welcome Team what you are hoping for, and Tia will suggest a starting point in your first conversation. Reiki is a common first session. ITA and IET tend to suit clients who want to work with a specific emotional theme.

### Can energy work replace therapy or medication?

No. Energy work is not a substitute for medical or mental health treatment. If you are working with a therapist, prescriber, or physician, keep that care in place. Energy work can sit alongside it, and with your permission Tia can coordinate with your other Pathways Within providers.

### What if I get emotional during a session?

That is common and welcome. Some clients cry, laugh, or feel a wave of relief. Tia holds the space, slows down if you need it, and checks in afterward. If something significant surfaces, she can help you bring it to your therapist or connect you with one.

### How often do people come?

It varies. Some clients book a few sessions close together during a hard stretch and then come monthly. Others come once in a while as a reset. Tia will share what she notices and let you set the pace.
