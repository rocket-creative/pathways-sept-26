# Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice is for Pathways Within - Wisdom and Wellness Collaborative. It describes how medical and mental health information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

## Who follows this notice

Pathways Within, its clinicians, prescribers, wellness practitioners, Welcome Team, and business associates. [NEEDS: legal entity name and the managed services organization structure, if it must be named]

## How we may use and disclose your health information

Treatment, payment, and health care operations; coordination between your Pathways Within providers with your consent; appointment reminders; as required by law; to prevent a serious threat to health or safety; and other uses listed in the full notice. Psychotherapy notes receive additional protection. [NEEDS: full text]

## Uses that require your written authorization

Marketing, sale of information, most uses of psychotherapy notes, and anything not described in this notice. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time.

## Your rights

To inspect and receive a copy of your record; to request corrections; to request restrictions; to request confidential communications; to receive an accounting of disclosures; to receive a paper copy of this notice; and to be notified of a breach of unsecured information.

You may file a complaint with our Privacy Officer [NEEDS: name and contact] or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
