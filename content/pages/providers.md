## Meet our therapy dogs

They help make a comfortable, cozy place where people can share their dreams, hopes, and feelings.

### Domino

Domino brings comfort with a calm presence, gentle eyes, and an intuitive heart. He is known for the best snuggles and quiet support, and he notices when someone needs a little extra love.

He may rest quietly during a session or offer a paw. He is a reminder of presence, patience, and unconditional affection: a good boy, and an even better therapist.

### Gypsy Sassafras

Gypsy Sassafras was born on a dairy farm in upstate New York on August 22, 2022. She lives in a beach community outside of New York City, where she walks the boardwalk, retrieves balls, and runs beside her mom on roller skates.

She sometimes pops up during a video session, and in the office she likes a belly rub and greets clients with a happy dance. She holds space for laughter and crying and comforts sensitive feelings. You can follow her on Instagram at @Gypsy_Sassafras.
