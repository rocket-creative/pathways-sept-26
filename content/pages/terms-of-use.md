# Website Terms of Use

Effective date: [NEEDS: date]. By using pathwayswithinwellness.com you agree to these terms. [NEEDS: attorney review before publication.]

## This site is not medical advice

The content on this site is educational. It describes services we offer and general information about mental health and wellness. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, or a substitute for care from a licensed provider. Reading a page does not create a provider client relationship. That begins when you complete an intake and consent to treatment.

## Emergencies

This website and its forms are not monitored around the clock. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Veterans: press 1) or call 911.

## Clinical review

Clinical pages name the licensed provider who wrote them and the clinician who reviewed them, with the date of the last review. We correct errors when we find them or when you report them to Welcome@pathwayswithin.com.
