# Psychiatric Medication Management on Long Island

Medication management at Pathways Within is psychiatric evaluation, prescribing, and follow up for adolescents ages 10 and up and adults. It is part of a whole person approach to mental health and emotional wellness. Each person's path is unique, and medication may be one supportive tool in helping you feel more grounded, balanced, and connected to yourself.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Veterans: press 1) or call 911.

## What medication management is

Medication management is ongoing care from a prescriber who assesses your symptoms, recommends medication when it fits, and monitors how it works over time. Your care plan may include starting a medication, continuing something that is already helping, adjusting a dosage, or exploring alternatives. Throughout the process, your prescriber monitors progress closely and makes changes as needed.

You may be wondering whether you really need medication. The answer depends on you, your symptoms, and your goals. For some people, medication helps quiet the intensity of anxiety, depression, mood changes, sleep difficulties, ADHD symptoms, or emotional overwhelm so they can feel more present in daily life and more available for therapy. For others, medication may not be the right next step. Our role is to help you explore your options with clarity, compassion, and support.

Our goal is not to change who you are. Our goal is to support your nervous system, your emotional well being, and your ability to feel more present in your life.

## Who it helps

Medication management may be helpful if you are dealing with:

- [Anxiety](/concerns/anxiety) and panic symptoms
- [Depression](/concerns/depression) ([NIMH on depression](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/depression))
- [ADHD](/concerns/adhd) in teens and adults ([NIMH on ADHD](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd))
- Mood changes, including [bipolar disorder](/concerns/bipolar-disorder)
- Stress and overwhelm
- Sleep disruption
- Emotional dysregulation
- Life transitions
- Medication changes or refills after a move or a change in providers
- Support alongside therapy or wellness care

Medication management is for adolescents ages 10 and up and for adults. For younger clients, a parent or guardian takes part in the first appointment and in ongoing decisions.

## What to expect in your first session at Pathways Within

Your first appointment is an opportunity to slow down and be heard. Your prescriber reviews your concerns, medical and mental health history, current medications or supplements, past treatment experiences, and your goals moving forward.

If medication is recommended, your prescriber explains the purpose, potential benefits, possible side effects, and what to expect as your body adjusts. You have space to ask questions and make informed decisions about your care. Not everyone leaves the first visit with a prescription, and that is fine.

Follow up appointments let your prescriber monitor how you are feeling, make thoughtful adjustments if needed, and continue supporting your progress over time. When starting or adjusting a medication, visits are closer together. Once things feel stable, they spread out.

## How care works here

At Pathways Within, we believe healing is relational, whole person, and deeply personal. Medication management often works best when it is part of a larger support system. For many clients, medication and therapy work together. Medication may help reduce the intensity of symptoms, while [therapy](/therapy) offers space to process, heal, build insight, and develop meaningful coping tools.

Every new client starts with a 360 intake with our Welcome Team. It is the same process for everyone. It does not diagnose. It records what you want and gives providers a baseline. If you want a prescriber, the team books one, and a therapist for [individual therapy](/therapy/individual-therapy) when you want that too. With your permission, your prescriber and therapist share notes so your care stays connected. Wellness services such as [acupuncture](/wellness/acupuncture) or [massage](/wellness/massage) can be added for sleep and tension. Read more at [How it works](/how-it-works).

Whether medication becomes a short term support during a difficult season or part of a longer term wellness plan, our team is here to walk alongside you with care, professionalism, and an understanding of the whole person.

## Where it is offered

Medication management is available in person at our Nassau offices and by telehealth when you are in New York and a video visit is available. The Welcome Team confirms the office and whether video prescribing applies to you.

## Who provides it

A psychiatric nurse practitioner on the Pathways Within team provides this care. Meet the team in the [provider directory](/providers).

Prescribing here looks at more than a symptom list. Relationships, home, culture, habits, and daily pressure all matter. Medication can be one part of treatment, alongside the changes, skills, and support that make improvement last.

## Common questions

### Why would I meet with a psychiatric nurse practitioner?

A psychiatric nurse practitioner is trained to assess mental health symptoms, provide a diagnosis when appropriate, and prescribe psychiatric medication. A visit can help if symptoms are affecting your mood, sleep, focus, relationships, or daily functioning, and you would like to explore whether medication may offer support.

### Will I be prescribed medication at my first appointment?

Not always. Your first appointment is focused on understanding your needs, history, symptoms, and goals. If medication seems appropriate, your prescriber discusses options with you, including potential benefits, risks, and side effects. Any medication plan should feel collaborative, informed, and aligned with your overall care.

### Do I need to be in therapy to receive medication management?

Medication management can be offered on its own, but many people benefit from combining medication with therapy. When you are working with a Pathways Within therapist, your prescriber collaborates with the team for a more connected care experience. If you are not currently in therapy, we can help you explore whether it may be helpful as part of your overall plan.

### How often will I need appointments?

The frequency depends on your needs and where you are in the medication process. When starting or adjusting medication, appointments may be more frequent so your prescriber can monitor how you are feeling and make changes safely. Once things feel more stable, follow up appointments may be spaced out.

### What if I am nervous about taking medication?

That is completely understandable. Many people have questions or concerns about psychiatric medication. Your prescriber takes time to discuss what you are feeling, answer your questions, and help you make decisions at a pace that feels comfortable. Medication is only one tool, and your voice is an important part of the process.

### Can my teenager be seen?

Yes. Medication management includes adolescents ages 10 and up. A parent or guardian attends the first visit and stays involved in decisions. Teens also get time to speak for themselves. If your teen is in therapy here, the providers coordinate with your consent.
