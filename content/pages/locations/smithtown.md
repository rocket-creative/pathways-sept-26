# Therapy and Wellness in Smithtown, NY

Pathways Within Smithtown is at 496 Smithtown Bypass, Suite 203, Smithtown, NY 11787. It is our Suffolk County hub and the home base for our Suffolk clinical team, led by Lee Wasser, LMHC-D, Clinical Director for Suffolk. Licensed therapists see adults, couples, teens, and children here. Our psychiatric nurse practitioner offers medication management, and massage and acupuncture appointments are available in the same suite. [NEEDS: confirm wellness services offered at Smithtown]

The office is on Route 347 (Smithtown Bypass), which makes it easy to reach from most of western and central Suffolk County.

## Common questions

### Is the Smithtown office wheelchair accessible?

Yes. Smithtown has an accessible entrance [NEEDS: confirm ground floor or elevator]. If you have specific access needs, tell the Welcome Team when you book. They will confirm the details before your first visit.

### Do you offer telehealth if I cannot get there?

Yes. Most of our therapists and our psychiatric nurse practitioner see clients by video in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida. Massage and acupuncture are in person only. Read more at [telehealth](/telehealth).

### Which insurance plans do you accept in Smithtown?
