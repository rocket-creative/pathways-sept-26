# Website Privacy Policy

Effective date: [NEEDS: date]. This policy covers information collected through the Pathways Within - Wisdom and Wellness Collaborative website, pathwayswithinwellness.com. It does not replace the [Notice of Privacy Practices](/notice-of-privacy-practices), which governs protected health information under HIPAA once you become a client.

[NEEDS: attorney review of this entire page before publication. The sections below are the factual scaffold; final language must come from counsel.]

## What we collect on this site

- Information you submit through our contact forms: name, email, phone, insurance plan name, office preference, services of interest, and what you are seeking support with. Forms are hosted by our practice management platform, Trust Driven Care, and delivered to the Welcome Team.
- Text messages you send to (631) 371-3825 and the chat widget, which route to the same platform.
- Standard web analytics: pages visited, device type, approximate location, and referral source. [NEEDS: name the analytics tool and whether IP anonymization is on]
- Cookies used for analytics and to remember your preferences. [NEEDS: cookie list]

## How we use it

To respond to you, schedule your intake, verify benefits you ask us to verify, and improve the site. We do not sell your information. We do not use form submissions for advertising audiences. [NEEDS: confirm no ad pixels; if any exist, disclose]

## Text messaging consent

By submitting a form or texting us, you consent to transactional messages such as appointment reminders and account notifications. Marketing messages are sent only if you check the marketing consent box. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

## Who we share with

Our practice management and messaging vendors, who process data on our behalf under agreements that require them to protect it; and anyone we are legally required to share with. [NEEDS: vendor list]

## Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete website submissions, by emailing Welcome@pathwayswithin.com. Clinical records follow the Notice of Privacy Practices instead.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them online. Parents and guardians contact us on behalf of minors.

## Changes

We will post the new effective date here when this policy changes.
